What is CometPark?

CometPark is a student initiative at UT Dallas, helping commuters find parking spots.

Why was CometPark Developed?

  1. We know how difficult parking can be during peak hours
  2. Students shouldn't have to waste time searching for a spot
  3. Making parking availability easier to access

How it works?

enter your permit type, destination
and arrival time to find
recommended parking areas.

Where the data comes from?

Parking availability is collected by checking how full each lot is at different times of day.

Students can also submit anonymous feedback about their recent parking experience to help expand data over time.
